About ToolShare

A community-run nonprofit making tools and knowledge accessible to everyone since 2013.

Our Mission

Tools for Everyone, Not Just Those Who Can Afford Them

ToolShare started in 2013 with 40 tools in a volunteer's garage. Today we operate a professional 4,000 sq ft facility with over 850 tools, a full workshop space, and a thriving community of makers, fixers, and learners.

We believe no one should have to buy a $300 drill they'll use once. Sharing resources builds community, reduces waste, and puts money back in your pocket.

Values

What Drives Us

Community First

Every decision is made with the neighborhood in mind, not profit.

Sustainability

Sharing tools reduces consumption, waste, and carbon footprint.

Accessibility

Income-based pricing ensures no one is priced out of participation.

Education

Teaching skills builds independence and confidence across generations.

History

Our Story

2013
Founded in a Garage
Started with 40 donated tools and 12 founding members. First "workshop" was a neighbor's driveway.
2015
First Dedicated Space
Moved into a 1,000 sq ft rented space on Elm Street. Membership grew to 120.
2017
Nonprofit Status
Obtained 501(c)(3) nonprofit status, enabling grant funding and tax-deductible donations.
2019
Workshop Program Launch
Started formal workshop programming with 6 classes in year one. 200+ attendees.
2021
New Facility
Moved to current 4,000 sq ft facility. Online reservation system launched.
2025
850+ Tools & 520 Members
Largest community tool library in the region. 48 workshops per year and growing.
